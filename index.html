SNEH INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL · ATL

ATAL TINKERING LAB

Imagine. Innovate. Tinker. A dedicated space at SNEH International School for students to explore ideas and learn through hands-on innovation.

Innovation starts with imagination

Albert Einstein rightly said, ‘Imagination is more important than knowledge’ and the concept of Atal Tinkering Lab is to encourage students to imagine and innovate.

Atal Innovation Mission is a flagship initiative set up by NITI Aayog to promote innovation and entrepreneurship across the country.

At SNEH International School, the ATL has been a platform for seminars, Tinker Fests and workshops since 2017.

Ideas from young innovatorsThe NIF archive brings together practical ideas developed by students and young innovators.
Student ideasIGNITE has featured student ideas covering everyday problems in homes, schools, farming and public places.
Start with a problemMany good ideas start with something a student notices in everyday life.
Try it outAt the ATL, students can sketch, build, test and improve their ideas.

Innovative examples from NIF.

Reversible benches at public places

Simran Chadha, Class 8

Simran Chadha proposed benches that can be turned with a handle so a dry or clean side is available after rain or use.

Looms for physically challenged

R. Elakkiya and R. Pavithra

The two sisters modified a traditional loom by replacing the foot pedal with a motor and gearbox, making it easier to operate without using the legs.

A colour-coded thermometer

Janmejay Singh Rathore

The idea uses colour to make temperature levels easier to understand, with extra guidance for people who may have difficulty reading a conventional thermometer.

Solar laminator

Amandeep Singh

A school student developed a laminator that uses focused sunlight for heating, with an electrical option when solar energy is unavailable.
